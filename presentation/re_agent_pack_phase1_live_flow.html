Business Reviewer — what they do

The reviewer agent runs in two modes, each invoked by the human operator. Step 5 stamps each rule as Priority or Standard; Step 8 gives a publication Go / No-go. Business stakeholders validate the generated Word documents directly, outside the repo.

Mode 1 — Grounding review (Step 5)
  • Rule count sanity check (are there enough rules for the number of source files?)
  • Coverage gap scan (any keyword-bearing functions without a rule card?)
  • Plain-English clarity check (does every card have a stakeholder-facing statement?)
  • Evidence resolution (does every cited file::function actually exist?)
Review Priority stamping (Step 5)

After grounding checks, every rule is automatically stamped as:

  • Priority — the rule is inferred, sample-derived, or ambiguous. Business reviewers should read closely.
  • Standard — the rule traces directly to legacy code with no open ambiguities. Business reviewers can skim.

There is no in-repo approval step. The Word documents generated in Step 7 are the review artifact sent to business stakeholders.

Example stamping output
R-001 — Client ID is required Grounding: [SG] · Ambiguities: none · Evidence: resolved → Review Priority: Standard R-007 — Cycle Code must match insurance line Grounding: [inferred] · Ambiguities: A-003 → Review Priority: Priority
Mode 2 — Publication sign-off (Step 8)
  • Go / No-go on the final deliverables before they are shared with a client audience.
  • Blocks publication if any placeholder-grounded item appears unqualified in a client-facing document.
